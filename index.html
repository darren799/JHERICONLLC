01 / WHO WE ARE

Mission-focused solutions. Measurable value.

JHERICON LLC is an IT and management consulting company focused on practical, client-centered solutions for federal, state, and local government customers, commercial organizations, and community partners.

Our capabilities span program and project management, IT governance, cybersecurity, data and software solutions, emerging technology, and professional development. We apply a solutions–performance–excellence approach across technology and workforce challenges.

Our complementary education mission seeks to expand affordable digital skills and career opportunities through hybrid learning. JHERICON is presently an LLC, not a registered nonprofit or tax-exempt organization.
