01 / WHO WE ARE

Technology should open doors.

JHERICON LLC is an IT-focused business with a dual purpose: delivering valuable technology consulting and training services while working toward broader access to affordable, practical technology education.

We envision a future where students, career changers, businesses, and underserved communities can gain the skills and support needed to thrive in a digital world.
