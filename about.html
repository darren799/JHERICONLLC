Where commercial solutions and community purpose meet.

01 / WHO WE ARE

Mission-focused solutions. Measurable value.

JHERICON LLC is a certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) and IT and management consulting company focused on practical, client-centered solutions for federal, state, and local government customers, commercial organizations, and community partners.

Our capabilities span program and project management, IT governance, cybersecurity, data and software solutions, emerging technology, and professional development. We apply a solutions–performance–excellence approach across technology and workforce challenges.

Our complementary education mission seeks to expand affordable digital skills and career opportunities through hybrid learning. JHERICON is presently an LLC, not a registered nonprofit or tax-exempt organization.

06 / OUR LONG-TERM VISION

Commercial strength.
Community purpose.

We believe a sustainable technology business can help create meaningful educational opportunities. Our long-term aspiration is to expand our community impact, explore affordable training for underserved learners, and pursue a nonprofit structure when appropriate.

Our current status: JHERICON LLC operates as a for-profit LLC. It is not currently a registered nonprofit or a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) organization. This website does not solicit charitable donations.
